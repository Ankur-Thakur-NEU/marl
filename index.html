The slot

The wall is full at the bottom and full at the top.

Under about $190, the hiker already has REI’s own jacket and Patagonia. Above $500, they are buying Arc’teryx or Gore. The open band is a judgment, not a quote: a humid-climate shell around $229–$279.

Matching Torrentshell’s $179–$189 from a cold start, at a few hundred pieces, is not a plan. India does not get to undercut Patagonia’s Vietnam volume.

What actually fails on the trail

Not a bigger millimeter number.

The face

Pack straps and cuffs shred light cloth. The useful class is a 40–70 denier nylon, in the neighborhood of Foray and Torrentshell.

The finish

PFC-free DWR is the law in California and New York. It also wears off. Pretending it lasts like the old chemistry is how returns start.

The seam

Tape peels. Zippers die at the slider. A waterproof cloth still leaks at every needle hole until it is taped.

The seat

Trail pants blow out where a hiker sits and steps. A pattern that only looks technical does not survive a week.

03 · The brand

3-layer shell

PFAS-free membrane and DWR. Cap hood. Pit zips. Judgment $229–$279. Under the Foray. Far under Gore.

Men’s and women’s are two blocks. A women’s pant is not a men’s pant shrunk on a photocopier.

Where it is sold

The brand’s own site, from the first unit.

Direct keeps the retail dollar. That is what makes a small run possible. A factory price that dies at wholesale can still work on the site.

A planning sketch, not a quote: around $70 to make a shell, sold near $249, can leave cash after cards, shipping, and returns. Nobody has quoted $70. Do not publish $249 because it looks tidy. REI is not the plan, and not the milestone.

Where it is cut

Indore can sew a pant. A taped shell is a different room.

The city’s woven shops already make shirts and trousers. That is the head start for the pant and the shirt. Seam tape needs a hot-air machine and a trained operator. The first shell samples may be developed at a plant that already tapes, then brought to Indore on our pattern.

India is the factory. It is not the story on the hangtag. No Himalayan costume. The founder stands on the line, tests in a real monsoon, and publishes the sheet.

The number that decides the price

Duty, not the wage, is the lever.

Water-resistant synthetic outerwear has cleared near 7.1%. Miss that test and the same family of garments has cleared near 27.7% or 27.9%. That spread is larger than an Indore-versus-Vietnam wage story.

7.1percent, if the garment passes the water-resistant test. A broker has to say so, this month, on these three constructions.

Country duty on Indian goods changed four times between August 2025 and July 2026. A July 2026 notice put an extra 10% on India. Reconfirm before costing. Nothing else is safe until that note exists.

What we will print

A number only after the test that produced it.

On the card

Fiber. Denier. PFAS-free, in writing. Hydrostatic head with the method, ISO 811, and the lot code. Where it was sewn. What a repair costs.

Not on the card

No Gore hangtag. No PFAS exemption. No Regenerative Organic mark on a nylon pant. No “UPF 50” copied off someone else’s hangtag. No lifetime warranty before we know the return rate.

Regenerative Organic is a farm certificate for cotton, hemp, or wool. The first pant is stretch nylon. Sustainability here is the chemistry we refuse, the test we publish, and a repair price.

The mark

MARL. One syllable. Rhymes with snarl.

Plain wide caps. No mountain, no badge, no chevron. Marlboro is the neighbor Americans will hear if the mark gets cute, so it does not.

marl.com is taken. marlequipment.com was unregistered on 18 October 2026 and may not be now. A web search is not a trademark clearance. A lawyer still has to look at US and India class 25 and 35, and at Marlboro and at TIBA + MARL, before a label is printed.
